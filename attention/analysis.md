# Analysis

## Layer 8, Head 11

This attention head appears to connect determiners and other noun modifiers to the noun they modify. In each of the sentences below, the words "the", "a" and "my" pay most of their attention to the noun that comes right after them.

Example Sentences:
- The cat sat on the [MASK].
- She gave me a [MASK] for my birthday.

## Layer 8, Head 10

This attention head appears to connect direct objects to the verbs that govern them. The object noun (or the word standing in for it) attends strongly to the verb whose action it receives.

Example Sentences:
- She opened the [MASK] and walked inside.
- The chef cooked a delicious [MASK] for dinner.
